About Lora Evaluate

  • Only the first Civitai image is saved next to the LoRA as name.preview.png (or jpg/webp). The others are view-only.
  • Opening a LoRA rechecks Civitai so previews and author text stay up to date.
  • Use ★ to favorite LoRAs. The eye icon hides a category and its LoRAs from All; 🗂 Categories builds your own from selected LoRAs.
  • Cache lives in local/ on localhost and/or lora_evaluate_cache.json in your library folders.
  • Works best in Chrome or Edge (File System Access API).
